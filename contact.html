Common questions

FAQs

How is a Cal Guard different from a boil-and-bite?

A boil-and-bite is a generic blank you soften in hot water and bite into. The fit depends entirely on how you bite it — and biting hard thins the material exactly where you need it most, across the front teeth. A Cal Guard starts from a digital scan of your actual teeth, so the fit and the material thickness are both controlled before it's ever manufactured.

How long does the whole process take?

The scan appointment takes about 15 minutes. Lab turnaround is measured in days rather than weeks, and your provider will confirm the exact timeline when you book. Scan early in the week and you're usually protected by the weekend.

What does a Cal Guard cost?

Pricing is set by each certified provider and depends on the tier you choose and any custom design work. Request a fitting and we'll share the exact cost with you before you commit to anything.

Can I get one if I wear braces?

Yes — and it matters more, not less. Orthodontic appliances increase the risk of soft-tissue injury during contact. Mention your braces when you request a fitting so your provider can plan for it, and expect to be rescanned if your treatment changes your bite significantly.

Will it stop a concussion?

No mouthguard should be sold to you as concussion prevention, and we won't do it. The research on mouthguards and concussion is genuinely mixed. What's well established is that a properly fitted guard substantially reduces dental and soft-tissue injury. We go into detail on The Science page.

What if the fit isn't right?

Bring it back to your provider. Minor adjustments happen in the chair. If it still isn't right, we remake it, and if we can't get it right we refund you. That's the whole guarantee — no fine print games.

Can you fit a whole team?

Yes. We run on-site scanning days for schools, clubs and gyms, with team colors, numbers and logos on every guard and consolidated billing. Get in touch using the form above and pick "Team or bulk order".

How do I look after it?

Rinse it in cool water after every use, brush it occasionally with a soft brush, and store it dry in its case. Keep it away from hot water, dashboards and direct sun — heat is the one thing that will warp a custom fit.
