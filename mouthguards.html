What Goes Into Every Guard

Every Cal Guard starts as a digital scan of your teeth and ends as a piece of equipment made for one mouth only — yours.

Digital Scan Accuracy

An intraoral scan captures your bite to within fractions of a millimetre — no trays, no gag reflex, no distortion.

Controlled Thickness

We set material thickness digitally before manufacturing, so the protection is exactly where the impact lands.

Days, Not Weeks

Scan on Monday, play protected by the weekend. Our lab turnaround is built around real season schedules.

Retention Without Clenching

A guard that fits stays put on its own. No jaw tension, no constant readjusting mid-play.

Comfort You'll Keep In

Slim profiles that let you breathe and talk normally — the single biggest reason athletes actually wear them.

Your Colors, Your Name
