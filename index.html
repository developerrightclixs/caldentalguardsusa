The anterior ramp

Designed for Power & Balance

A mouthguard sits between your arches, which means it decides how your jaw closes. Most guards leave that to chance. Ours doesn't.

The patented anterior ramp is the raised contour running across the front of every Cal Guard. Instead of a flat biting surface, the ramp gives the lower jaw a defined place to land — so the bite settles the same way every time, rather than being clenched into position.

That consistency is what reduces the jaw and neck tension athletes carry through a game, and it's built into the guard before it's ever manufactured. Breathing and speech stay completely natural.
