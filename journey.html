Why we do it

Protection Shouldn't Be a Luxury

We exist to make sure every athlete — whatever their sport, age or budget — can compete with real protection in their mouth.

A knocked-out tooth follows someone for life. The treatment costs compound for decades. And the thing standing between an athlete and that outcome is often a $3 guard sitting unused at the bottom of a gym bag.

We built Cal Guards to close that gap: a guard good enough that athletes choose to wear it, made accessible enough that teams and families can actually get one.
