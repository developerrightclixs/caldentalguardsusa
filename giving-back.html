Our commitment to the community

Giving Back

Protecting young athletes. Strengthening the communities we serve.

Cal Guards isn't just about selling mouthguards — it's about protecting kids.

Why this matters to us

Our Mission

Every child deserves the protection, the confidence and the chance to play — not just the ones whose families can afford a custom guard.

We spend our days building mouthguards for athletes who compete at the highest level. The same craft, the same materials and the same lab belong to the kid playing Saturday-morning youth football just as much as they belong to a professional.

So alongside the business, we're committing Cal Guards to the work of giving back — partnering with local nonprofits, schools and youth sports programs to put properly fitted protection in the mouths of kids who would otherwise go without.

Our goal is simple: a quality mouthguard for every child in our communities, regardless of their financial situation.

Why access matters

A single knocked-out permanent tooth can cost $20,000+ to treat over a lifetime.

That bill follows a child into adulthood. A properly fitted guard costs a small fraction of it — and no kid should go without one because of what their family can afford.
